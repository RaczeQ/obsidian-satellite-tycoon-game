You start a game as a founder of the satellite manufacturer and operator on [[Earth]]. 

At first your company specialises in optical measurements and the payload (satellites) are launched by another company.

With time and resources, you start working on your own dedicated rocket launching facility, develop other measurement instruments (SAR, Internet connection, navigation satellites, space exploration) and have an RnD department. Those aspects are tightly combined with the technology tree and unlocking more parts of the game.

Parts of the research enables sending satellites to other celestial bodies and planets:
- [[Moon]]
- [[Mars]]
- [[Jupyter]]
- Outer space

Long term goal: send 1 000 satellites into the orbit around Earth for passive income for global satellite network coverage (Starlink equivalent)

After the [[Solar System]] becomes dominated with your company's technologies and services, you are given a contract to take a part in the expedition to another galaxy - [[Andromeda Galaxy]].

---

In the [[Andromeda Galaxy]], your task is to scan and evaluate viability of potential systems and planets for colonisation.

After successfully finding the place for the first colony to be created (player will have a choice, but it will just change the skin/color on the planet), your next task will be to explore the planets (atmosphere monitoring, weather probing, communication satellites, mapping the surface etc - basically the same as on [[Earth]]).

There will also be a separate path for exploring other planets in the system and you will encounter [[Ancient Artefacts]] that was left by some extinct alien race.

Long term goal: send 5 000 excavation drones to the archeological sites to repair the artefacts and reboot them.

Activating the last artefact opens the wormhole which you can use and it turns out that it will move you into the [[Sci-Fi Future World]].

---

In the [[Sci-Fi Future World]], the humanity is an intra-galactic race and have diplomatic relationships with alien races (sort of like The Citadel in Mass Effect).

There is an a first major collaboration between species and their goal is to create the [[Massive Quantum Super Computer]]. After initial tests it became obvious that the power draw of the full cluster will be impossible to sustain. As the parallel project, The Dyson Sphere is suggested as the power source solely dedicated to powering the Quantum Computer.

You company is tasked with building the solar panels and launch them into the star's orbit and create the core of the Dyson Sphere.  

Long term goal: send 10 000 satellites to build a Dyson Sphere and power the largest quantum computer in the known universe.

---

You are a traveller from the other universe and arrive in the [[Magic Fantasy World]]. All you know is your experience and knowledge from your world - especially how to run the satellite business.

After moving through the teleporter, you land at the edge of the forest near the path. Disoriented, you see an old man with long white hair and beard wearing a gray robe and a hat riding a cart (easter egg to the Gandalf from the Middle-Earth). He takes you on the cart and you ask about this world. He is confused at first, seeing a stranger knowing nothing about this world, but decides to help you and takes you to his home in the city.

There you learn more about this world and about the wizard. He learns more about you and the universe you came from. When you ask if they have any maps of this world, he seems confused, because no one is using anything like that in this universe. 

You ask him how does navigation work in this world. He explains that most of the places are connected with magical portals and they connect "named places" that are used as waypoints for everyone. There are also 5 different realms and we are currently in the [[First Realm]]. Portals can also connect places between realms and when you want to move somewhere, you just have to follow [[Threadways]], a network of portals and travel hubs named [[Anchor|Anchors]] - you recognise the similarity with the metro system and its stations.

You explain to him what a map is - a view from the top showing roads, landmarks, rivers, forests etc. You can use it to measure the exact distance between points. Wizard explains that no one needs to know how far something is, because portals are instantaneous and you can move very quickly through the world.

After that, the wizard suggests you something crazy - Do you want to see this realm from the top? We can fly a dragon and see it!

So you mount the dragon together, you take your pen and a notebook and draw a simple map of the area around the city where the wizard lives. After landing, the wizard acknowledges that this can in fact be useful.

So together you start a mapping agency - first you use the help of a single dragon and the wizard's granddaughter who is an artist, flies the dragon whom she knows since she was little and draw maps for customers.

You quickly become successful and have to buy more dragons from some trainers and also artists. Next step is to have your own hatching facility where you hatch, and train dragons to expand your services. You also have a training academy for the cartographers. From basic map charting, to mapping the sources of different magical components with special wizards who are also trained to be cartographers.

There is also a plan to send dragons for long high altitude flights with telepathic connection between the wizard and the dragon (requires special hatching and training as a dragon-wizard pair) for mapping bigger areas as well as doing astrology observations.
