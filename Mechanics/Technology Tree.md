## Research Mechanics

### Unlock Conditions

- Most research is locked behind **story progression** or **universe unlocks**
- Some branches require prerequisites (finish Earth network before Andromeda)
- Research costs scale with technology level

### Companion Guidance

- Companions (scientists, wizards, etc.) provide hints on what to research next
- Dialogues unlock new research branches

### Research Efficiency

- Faster research time with upgraded Laboratory
- Some technologies give research bonuses
