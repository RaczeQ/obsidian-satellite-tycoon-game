# Currency

This page describes the currency systems across all universes.

## Currency Rules

- **Normal currency** (Coins) is **siloed per universe**. Progress in Earth cannot be used in Andromeda, Sci-Fi, or Fantasy.
- **Premium currency** is **shared across all universes**. Earned slowly through normal play, can be used for time-skips and cosmetics only.
- **No predatory progression gates** - you can complete all content without spending premium currency.
- Achievements that require spending premium currency are still grindable (e.g. "Buy 5 skins").

## Earth / Solar System

### Coins (Primary)
Earned from:
- Completing missions (science data delivery, resource collection)
- Small passive income from the Earth satellite internet coverage network (1,000 satellites target)

Uses:
- Building satellite components
- Launches (rocket fuel)
- Hiring research scientists (Laboratory)
- Buying orbital capacity slots

### Premium Currency: Bux
Earned from:
- Daily events
- Random missions (chance drop)
- Leveling up
- Watching ads (2 Bux per ad)

Uses:
- **Time-skips only** (no progression gates)
  - Instant production complete
  - Instant launch
  - Instant satellite recharge
- **Cosmetics only** (no progression)
  - Satellite/skin packs
  - Station visual themes

**Important**: Bux **cannot** be used to:
- Build satellites or buy components
- Increase mission capacity
- Unlock technology
- Buy orbital slots

## Andromeda Galaxy

### Coins (Primary)
Earned from:
- Completing planet missions (atmosphere scans, life detection)
- Reactivating ancient artifacts
- Planet colonization contracts

Uses:
- Building drone components
- Launch facilities
- Hiring Xeno-Ops technicians
- Buying orbital capacity slots

---

## Sci-Fi Future World

### Coins (Primary)
Earned from:
- Satellite construction contracts
- Working on Dyson Sphere projects
- Quantum computer assistance

Uses:
- Building high-tech satellite components
- Fusion reactor construction
- Quantum communication hardware
- Buying orbital capacity slots

---

## Magic Fantasy World

### Coins (Primary)
Earned from:
- Map commissions from clients
- Wayglass beacon sales
- Dragon breeding and services
- Artisan dragon services

Uses:
- Buying stable space
- Hiring dragon trainers/cartographers
- Purchasing dragon equipment
- Expanding Wayglass network
- Unlocking realm portal access

---

## Currency Exchange (Optional)

Players may choose to convert shared Bux into any universe's local currency:
- 1 Bux = X Earth Coins (rate varies)
- 1 Bux = Y Andromeda Coins (rate varies)
- 1 Bux = Z Sci-Fi Coins (rate varies)
- 1 Bux = W Fantasy Coins (rate varies)

**Rules**:
- Exchangeable only when player is online
- Exchange rates fluctuate slightly (±5%)
- Exchange is a **one-time decision** per universe per session
- Cannot reverse the exchange

This allows players to use Bux to accelerate progress in a specific universe they're currently focused on, without breaking the core economy design.
